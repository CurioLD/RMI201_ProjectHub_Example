RMI101 · RMIT Online business courses

Project charter

Accredited business courses for RMIT Online, designed with the ABLE approach, built in Canvas and handed over in two tranches.

Sources: RMI101 project charter (kick-off deck, 24 August 2026) and Statement of Work 2 (signed 31 August 2026). Reference document.

In this charter

01Overview

Curio Group is designing and building business courses for RMIT Online: accredited refreshes of existing courses and one full development. Every course uses the Activity-Based Learning Experience (ABLE) approach, is built in Canvas with LTI integration in RMIT Online visual style, and is tested to WCAG 2.2 AA. Content is drafted with Curio-approved AI tools, with human judgement at every stage of design, QA and compliance review.

The question to be answered

How do we deliver market-ready business courses for RMIT Online that lift the student experience and hand over on time?

Key project steps

  • Align: kick-off, technical and brand intake, blueprint workshop, then Gateway 1 blueprint review
  • Design: weekly storyboard authoring, SME review, remediation, video and graphics, then Gateway 2 detailed design review
  • Build: Canvas build, H5P and interactive elements, client environment QA and remediation, then Gateway 3 final sign-off
  • Launch: handover to RMIT Online internal teams, and a retrospective
  • Retain: two weeks of post go-live hot fix and remediation support

Deliverables

  • Courses built in Canvas LMS, in RMIT Online visual style, tested to WCAG 2.2 AA
  • ABLE learning modules with three to five weekly learning activities per module
  • Three project-based authentic assessments per course, within the existing Course Learning Outcomes
  • A weekly rich media video plus a Module 0 welcome video per course, AI-generated with human review and captioned to WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Full storyboard documentation, three QA gateways per course and a handover pack

Critical path assumption

RMIT Online provides consolidated feedback within two business days at every gateway. Any delay to gateway approvals compresses later stages and may mean renegotiating the handover date.

04People and governance

Stakeholders

  • RMIT Online operations: Mitzi Zagon, Head of Operations, owns gateway sign-off and delivery acceptance
  • RMIT course coordinators and SMEs: content accuracy, assessment design and storyboard review, one consolidated round per gateway
  • RMIT Online course design team: weekly governance check-ins and build environment QA
  • RMIT students: the reason the courses are being refreshed. CES scores and open feedback are the measure
  • Curio delivery team: learning design, build, QA and accessibility

Meetings

  • Course design blueprinting: Curio learning design team and RMIT Online course design team, once per course
  • Governance: Curio Project Director with RMIT Online, fortnightly
  • SME storyboard review: Curio learning design team and RMIT Online course design team, per module, two business days
  • Curio internal delivery stand-up: Curio project team, Tuesdays and Fridays

06Success measures
  • Every course hands over on its contracted date, with no renegotiated dates
  • Every course clears Gateway 3 with WCAG 2.2 AA verified and the Canvas and LTI build tested in the RMIT Online environment
  • One consolidated feedback round and one remediation round per gateway, with no extra review rounds needed
  • All modules, assessments and videos delivered in full, within the fixed fee
  • BUSM2639 CES scores improve against the current baseline, with the assessment instruction feedback visibly closed out
  • RMIT Online internal teams can run and maintain every course after the two-week handover, without Curio
  • The post go-live hot fix window closes with no material defects outstanding
07Scope, dependencies and risks

Boundaries

  • Work sits within the existing Course Learning Outcomes. Anything needing re-accreditation is out of scope
  • RMIT Online visual style using existing templates and asset libraries. No new brand or template development
  • Video is AI-generated with human review. Traditional production is quoted separately
  • One remediation round per gateway. Extra rounds are quoted on the standard rate card

Dependencies

  • Canvas access and LTI credentials at the start of each tranche
  • Source courses, course maps, Course Guides, CES feedback and the scope change documents
  • SME availability, and consolidated feedback within two business days at every gateway
  • Existing graphics and visual assets released for reuse

Risks

  • Gateway feedback beyond two business days compresses later stages and may move a handover date
  • BUSM3125 had no Course Coordinator until one was nominated; Margaret Heffernan returns from leave on 8 November
  • Jessica Borg was on parental leave until 12 October, across BUSM4497 Gateway 1
  • Tranche 1 runs courses in parallel. Curio was confirming extra design capacity before Gateway 2
